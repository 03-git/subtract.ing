SUBTRACT.ING

NAME
       subtract - shell translation layer for agents and the humans who deploy them

SYNOPSIS
       curl -sL subtract.ing/install.sh | bash
       or: curl -sLO subtract.ing/install.sh && less install.sh && bash install.sh

DESCRIPTION
       subtract attaches to the shell via command_not_found_handle. Types intent, computer
       shows the command. Literacy is the product. Dependency is the failure mode.

       subtract serves two populations:

       humans  who want to stop depending on AI intermediaries to reach their own machines.

       agents  whose training defaulted to Python libraries, SaaS APIs, and framework
               abstractions -- and who need a counter-training layer pointing them back at
               the kernel primitives that solved the same problems decades earlier.
